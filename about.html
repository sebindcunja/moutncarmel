About Us

Welcome to Mount Carmel Adult Day Program

Operated by The Congregation of Carmelite Sisters Institute of Our Lady of Carmel

Mount Carmel Adult Day Program provides a safe, welcoming, and compassionate environment where older adults can enjoy meaningful social interaction, engaging recreational activities, nutritious meals, and personalized support. Our goal is to promote independence, dignity, and an enhanced quality of life for every participant.

Inspired by the Carmelite tradition of faith, compassion, and service, our dedicated team creates opportunities for friendship, wellness, creativity, and community while also offering valuable respite and peace of mind for families and caregivers.

Who We Are

Guided by Compassion, Driven by Care

At Mount Carmel Adult Day Program, our mission and values inspire everything we do, creating a welcoming environment where seniors feel respected, supported, and connected.

Our Mission

Our Adult Day Centre provides a variety of daily activities that encourage stimulation, social interaction, and meaningful engagement. We strive to create an enjoyable day while promoting each participant's physical, emotional, and intellectual well-being in a safe and caring environment.

Our Values

We believe that by providing compassionate care, encouragement, and ongoing support, every participant can live with greater independence, dignity, confidence, and a strong sense of belonging within our community.
