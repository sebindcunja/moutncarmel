Daily Programs

Meaningful Activities Every Day

Every day is thoughtfully planned to encourage social interaction, creativity, physical wellness, and spiritual enrichment in a caring and welcoming environment.

Religious Gatherings

Prayer services, Rosary, spiritual reflection and community worship.

Arts & Crafts

Creative art sessions that inspire imagination and self-expression.

Flower Arrangements

Rosary making and flower arrangement activities promoting creativity.

Interaction Groups

Conversation groups encouraging friendship and communication.

Singing Groups

Enjoy music, singing, laughter and memorable moments together.

Bingo & Games

Fun indoor games that stimulate the mind while building friendships.

Exercise Classes

Gentle fitness sessions supporting mobility, balance and wellness.

Birthday Celebrations

Special celebrations to honour every participant's birthday.

Religious Holidays

Celebrating important religious feasts and seasonal traditions.

And Many More Activities...

Every month brings new experiences including seasonal celebrations, guest entertainers, educational sessions, cultural activities, community outings, games, wellness programs and many opportunities to connect with others in a warm and caring environment.
