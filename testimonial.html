Testimonial

Trusted By Thousands Of People And Nonprofits

Real stories from the families we've had the privilege of serving.

If you are looking for a Catholic adult day program that is caring and nurturing, consider the Mount Carmel Adult Day Program. It serves as a welcoming community for seniors on a beautiful 2 acre property in Woodbridge. The seniors who attend are lovingly and gently cared for by both the Sisters of Mount Carmel and the many volunteers. My mind is at ease because I know my mom is able to socialize with people her age in a safe environment. The program offers may activities including daily exercises, art activities, singing, the mass, the rosary and most importantly the Eucharist. Meals and snacks are carefully prepared and enjoyed by all. The program is run both in English and Italian. I am truly grateful I found this program for my mom as it helps her to age gracefully surrounding by caring friends and staff.

Anna Ellero
Family Member

Thank you for providing our seniors with the best care possible. Mount Carmel Adult Program is such a life saver to caregivers taking care of their senior parent(s). Seniors have the pleasure of hearing mass, doing arts and crafts and play games.

The meals and snacks provided are homemade. Every afternoon when I pick up my father he raves at how good lunch was. The home is clean, safe and I trust that my father is well taken care of.

All of the sisters and volunteers truly care for the seniors.

I trust my father in your care and I truly appreciate all that you do for him. Since he has been coming to Mount Carmel I have noticed a big change in his mood. He looks forward to coming and spending the day with all of wonderful sisters, staff and adult friends he has made at the centre.

Thank you for everything you are doing for our seniors and know that having Mount Carmel Adult Program for is truly a blessing for the seniors in addition to the caregivers.

With warm regards,

From the bottom of my heart, I want to thank the Sisters of Mount Carmel Day Program for the extraordinary love, compassion, and dignity they gave my beloved mom during the three years she attended your program.

Caring for someone with dementia is one of life's greatest privileges, but also one of its greatest challenges. From the very first day, we knew Mom was in a place where she was genuinely loved and cherished. The warmth, kindness, and patience shown by every Sister and volunteer gave our family an incredible sense of peace.

Your program was so much more than a safe place, it became a second home. Mom was surrounded by friendship, laughter, prayer, music, and delicious home-cooked meals. Every detail reflected love, compassion, and respect for each person in your care.

One memory that will stay with me forever is how my mom would often hug the Sisters before leaving each day. Those heartfelt embraces spoke volumes about the love and trust she felt. It was clear she wasn't simply attending a program, she had become part of a family.

The respite your program gave our family was an immeasurable gift, allowing us to recharge while knowing Mom was safe, happy, and deeply cared for. That peace of mind is something we can never repay.

Although my beloved mom has now passed, our hearts will always hold profound gratitude for the compassion, devotion, and humanity you showed her every single day. You brought joy, comfort, and dignity to the final years of her life, and for that, we will be forever grateful.

With deepest gratitude,
Piera Graci

It is hard to express how grateful I am for the love and care my mother received at the Carmelite Sisters' Adult Day Program. Before she passed away, she attended two or three days each week, and those days brought her so much joy. She truly loved being there.

The program offered far more than activities. It provided a community rooted in faith, kindness and compassion. Beginning each day with the Rosary and living out Catholic teachings gave my mom peace and purpose, and it was a great comfort to our family to know she was surrounded by people who shared her faith.

She enjoyed the arts and crafts, outdoor walks when the weather allowed, and especially the wonderful homemade Italian meals and snacks. Every detail reflected the care that made the program feel like home.

What touched us most were the wonderful volunteers and of course the Sisters. They welcomed my mother with warmth, patience, and genuine affection, always treating her with dignity and making her feel valued. Their kindness was a true reflection of Christ's love in action.

As her daughter, there was no greater gift than knowing she was safe, engaged, and happy in their care. The friendships she made and the joy she experienced enriched her day to day life. Our family will always be thankful to the Carmelite Sisters and everyone who makes this special ministry possible. Their faith, compassion, and dedication are a blessing to seniors and their families, and I wholeheartedly recommend this wonderful program to anyone seeking a loving, faith-filled environment for their loved one. It will always hold a special place in my heart.

Our family will always be grateful for the love and care my mom received at the Our Sisters of Mount Carmel Adult Day Program. Living with dementia can be challenging, but for three years this program gave her a safe, welcoming, and engaging place where she truly looked forward to spending her days.

She attended Monday through Friday, and every day was filled with meaningful social, recreational, and wellness activities that kept her active and connected. The Sisters of Mount Carmel and the dedicated volunteers treated her with incredible kindness, compassion, patience, and respect. They made every participant feel valued and cared for.

The meals were another wonderful part of the program. Each day they served breakfast, a delicious homemade lunch, and an afternoon snack, all prepared with the same warmth and attention they showed in every aspect of their care.

As a caregiver, knowing my mom was in such a loving and supportive environment gave our family tremendous peace of mind. This program enriched her life and made a meaningful difference in ours. We wholeheartedly recommend the Our Sisters of Mount Carmel Adult Day Program to any family looking for compassionate, high-quality care for a loved one.

The Sisters provided a wonderful, engaging routine filled with daily Mass, prayer times, arts and crafts, movement, gardening, and even cooking, alongside exceptional meals and snacks. Mount Carmel gave me priceless peace of mind and gave my mom a joyful, safe haven.
