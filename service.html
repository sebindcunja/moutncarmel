Services Offered

Compassionate Care & Engaging Programs

We provide a variety of supportive services and engaging activities that promote independence, wellness, dignity, and quality of life for older adults.

Nutritious Meals & Snacks

Diet-controlled, full-course hot lunches and nutritious snacks are served daily to support healthy living and individual dietary needs.

Social & Recreational Activities

Multi-lingual social, recreational, psycho-social, spiritual, physical, and cultural activities designed to keep participants active, engaged, and connected.

Health Education

Living and Learning educational sessions promoting healthy eating, wellness, disease prevention, and healthy lifestyle choices.

Medication & Nursing Support

Medication reminders and daily nursing services are available to support participants' health and overall well-being.

Community Bus Trips

Organized outings and bus trips to local attractions and places of interest provide enjoyable experiences and community engagement.

Personal Care Services

Occasional on-site hairdressing and barber services help participants maintain personal grooming and confidence in a comfortable setting.

Mount Carmel Adult Day Program

Operated by the Congregation of Carmelite Sisters Institute of Our Lady of Carmel, providing compassionate care, engaging activities, and meaningful support for seniors in our community.
